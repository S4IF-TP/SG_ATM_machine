ATM Machine – Your Digital Banking Companion

ATM Machine is a modern frontend banking application that simulates the experience of using a real ATM, right from your browser. Designed with a clean and intuitive interface, this app allows users to manage their finances securely and effortlessly.

Key Features:

💰 View Budget: Instantly check your current balance and manage your personal budget.

📜 Transaction History: Access a detailed log of all your past transactions with timestamps and descriptions.

💸 Send Money: Easily transfer funds to other accounts with just a few clicks.

🏦 Take a Loan: Request and manage loans directly within the app.

❌ Delete Account: Securely close your account if needed
